# Layout and whitespace (PEP 8)
Applies to: function_length

PEP 8 layout rules: indent with 4 spaces, never tabs; keep lines to 79 characters (99 is a common team limit); put two blank lines around top-level functions and classes and one blank line between methods; put imports at the top of the file, grouped as standard library, third-party, then local. Consistent layout makes problems in the logic easier to spot.
